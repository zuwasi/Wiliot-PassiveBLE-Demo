Step 2: The Paper

PassiveBLE: Towards Fully Commodity-Compatible BLE Backscatter

Dong et al. · ACM MobiCom 2025 · arXiv 2503.11490

What They Did

Built the first BLE backscatter system that establishes real BLE connections (on data channels) — not just one-shot advertising packets. Compatible with unmodified smartphones.

Key Innovation

A distributed coding scheme that offloads channel encoding to the excitation source via XOR operations on RF signals — so tags stay ultra-low-power while generating full BLE packets.

kbps goodput (LE 2M)
63.3×

9.9

Three Technical Breakthroughs

⚡ Synchronization

Coherent-detection circuit wakes tags with 93 ns accuracy and -30 dBm sensitivity. Enables symbol-level timing for BLE data packets.

91% @ -30 dBm 2.5 m range

🔀 Distributed Coding

XOR on RF signals: excitation source handles dynamic parameters, tags just scatter. Full BLE encoding at 9.9 µW.

No decoding on tag XOR on RF

📡 Connection Scheduler

Manages real BLE connections — establishment, maintenance, termination, frequency hopping — for multiple tags simultaneously.

>99.9% success TDD multi-tag

✅ What Works

  • 32 tags at ≤1 m/s → >95% read rate
  • ASIC tags self-powered within 1.5 m of reader
  • BLE data connections = authenticated reads
  • Works with unmodified smartphones

⚠️ Limitations Found

  • 64+ tags at >2 m/s → read rate drops below 80%
  • COTS prototype (491 µW) needs external power beyond 0.5 m
  • NLoS reduces goodput by ~15-30%
  • Max distance 17 m limits outdoor use

What We Learned

Technical Insights

  • BLE data channels change everything — advertising-only backscatter was a dead end for supply chain (15 kbps max)
  • ASIC vs COTS is a 50× gap — 9.9 µW vs 491 µW determines whether RF harvesting is feasible
  • Multi-tag scaling is the bottleneck — TDD scheduling limits practical deployments to ~32 tags per excitation source
  • Distance and speed are coupled — wider doors or slower speed buy proportional dwell time

Process Insights

  • Paper → Product in one session — the Amp workflow compressed weeks of R&D exploration into hours
  • Validation is non-negotiable — every model claim is spot-checked against paper data with tolerances
  • Interactive controls reveal limits — the Manipulate exposed the 64-tag / 2 m/s cliff before any physical prototype
  • Reusable packages compound value — PassiveBLE.wl can power future simulations without rebuilding
